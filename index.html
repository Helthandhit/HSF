Our Mission

Health, Wellbeing, Affordability & Community

We offer a range of activities, classes & courses at affordable prices. It’s our mission to build a health & wellbeing community that is inclusive for all.

We are building a friendly, ‘all are welcome’ environment. Our doors are open wherever you are in your journey.

We are community-led. We care about our members and the community we live and work in.

It’s our mission to make an impact.

About Us

High Street Fitness and Wellness is a modern & unique facility that offers a wide range of health and wellbeing activities, courses & classes for all ages and abilities. We use an affordable pricing structure in a friendly and welcoming environment because we want this gym to be different. We want this gym to be owned and run by the community and its users.

We firmly believe the concept of POW

Participation, Ownership and Warrantee.

When we include our users in all aspects of the gym, they feel ownership. When you feel involved & valued, we know we can achieve amazing results.

Our aim at High Street Fitness is simple, to improve the health, fitness and wellness of the local community residents.
