24hr Access

Dear Member,

We're thrilled to have you as a member of High Street Fitness, and we can't wait to see you crushing your fitness goals in our gym. As you might already know, we use a door app to grant access to our front doors outside of staffed hours.

Please use the member screen in this preview, or ask the front desk for a hand. Access is connected to your monthly membership, so it will not open the door if the membership is not active.

To keep members safe, tailgating is not allowed, and your membership is for your use only. When the door unlocks you have a few seconds to come in before it locks again. The front doors are covered by cameras for the safety of members.

If you have any questions about accessing the gym, ask the team at George Street.

Best regards,
Team High Street Fitness
