About Us

Run by the Community for the Community.

We are a charitable organisation on a mission to improve the health and wellbeing of the community by providing wellness hubs in the centre of towns that can easily be accessed by the community.

Our Vision

This is not just a “gym”. We want to work with like-minded people and support local business. The facility will host other businesses, provide training and qualifications, opportunities to volunteer, work, and access mental health support. We want this, our first facility, to be yours.

We are proudly trying to be as carbon neutral as possible. From our drive on zero single-use plastics to our unique training and qualifications programme, we support your mental health as much as your physical health.
