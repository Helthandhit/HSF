Our Project

High Street Fitness. George Street, Pontypool

Dream it.

It all begins with an idea. Ours; to regenerate the High Street, to make health and wellbeing affordable and for everyone. This was what our maiden project looked like when we moved in. Oh, and this was only the shop floor!

Build it.

But with some hard work and determination we have transformed the premises into something the community can be proud of.

Achieve it.

Now we have built somewhere for the community, run by the community.

Here are our achievements so far

  • Welsh Gym of the Year
  • Welsh Fitness Champions
  • Welsh Entrepreneur of the Year
  • Successful businesses operate with us
  • High quality personal trainers
  • Fitness classes
  • Most affordable gym around.
